Prices shown on the website are introductory starting prices. Larger or more complex requests may require a separate quote. Any additional work should be agreed to before it begins. Payment terms may be confirmed with the customer before service starts.

If a session is canceled before work begins, no service charge should apply unless a separate agreement says otherwise. If work has already started, payment may be due for the time or work already completed. Any refund decision for completed work will be handled case by case.
