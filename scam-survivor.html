How to Spot Common Scams

Use these rules during the game and in real life.

✉

Email

Check the full sender address, inspect links, and be suspicious of urgent password or payment requests.

💬

Text Messages

Do not use unexpected delivery or banking links. Open the official app or website yourself.

☎

Phone Calls

Caller ID can be spoofed. Hang up and call the organization using a trusted number.

🔐

Security Codes

Never give a caller a one-time passcode. Legitimate support teams should not ask for it.

🌐

Websites

A padlock only means the connection is encrypted. Verify the exact domain before entering information.

🔎

When Unsure

Choose “Verify First.” Use an official app, bookmark, bill, card, or organization website.
